# SheHealth

SheHealth is a women-focused health and nutrition web application designed
especially for working women and pregnant women.

## Problem

Working and pregnant women often face difficulty maintaining a balanced
nutrition routine because of busy schedules, lack of personalized guidance,
and limited access to simple nutrition information.

## Solution

SheHealth provides a simple and user-friendly platform for:

- Personalized nutrition guidance
- Meal planning
- Pregnancy trimester-based meal suggestions
- Hydration tracking
- Wellness and mood tracking
- Food and nutrition information
- Profile-based recommendations

## Target Users

- Working women
- Pregnant women

## Technology Stack
